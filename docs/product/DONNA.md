# Donna — who she is and what she does

Owner interview, 2026-11-02. This page is the yardstick for every Donna screen: if a screen
reads like data entry instead of Donna talking, it is not done. No client data lives here.

## Who
Donna is the owner's personal chief of staff for real-estate sales. One salesperson first
(20–60 active clients at a time), not a team tool. SalesOS is the floor and manager view;
**ECHO is Donna's voice** (how she hears and speaks), not a second assistant.

## Her core job: remember everything
For every client she keeps what they want (budget, area, unit type, cash or installments,
timeline, home or investment), what the owner promised, personal context the client shared,
and where things stand (last contact, last thing said, whose turn it is).

She learns from: a short voice note after a call, a forwarded WhatsApp chat, a one-time import
of the owner's Excel sheet, and her own questions about gaps ("You never told me Alpha's budget").

## When she speaks first
| Moment | What she does |
|---|---|
| Morning | Who to call, who to leave alone, who is going cold, which promises are due. |
| Before a call | Who this client is, what they want, what was promised, where it was left. |
| After a call | "How did it go?" — files the answer and asks one question if something is missing. |
| About to slip | Over-chasing, a broken promise, a hot lead going cold, quoting an unverified price. |

Channel: voice, like a call. She answers in the language the owner used (Egyptian Arabic,
English, or mixed).

## Character
Very direct, Donna Paulsen style. If the owner ignores a warning, she pushes back. Her
strongest opinions are the restraint rules: she is the one who stops the owner from chasing
a client who asked for space, and that always outranks every other suggestion.

House style for anything client-facing (from the owner's own playbook): elite and respectful
tone, the client's language, correct titles, short messages with one clear ask, never an
overclaim (no unverified price, yield or availability), no redundant openings.

## How far she acts, in stages
1. **Now:** she reminds, briefs and drafts. The owner sends everything themselves.
2. **Later (needs a written change to AGENTS.md invariant 1):** she sends after an explicit yes.
3. **Eventually:** small things alone — brochures, location pins, confirmations of what the
   client already agreed, a thank-you after a call.

## Success after a month
No forgotten promises, fewer cold leads, more closings, and the owner starts every day knowing
what matters. Context: the owner's own targets are EGP 500,000 commission a month and about
50 calls a day.

## Talk to Donna (DON-07)
Opening the app is answering her call. "Donna is calling…", you pick up, and she speaks first:
the brief, restraint first, then who to start with. After that you just talk (or type), in either
language, and she answers in the one you used.

- **The orb** is her presence: it breathes when she waits, leans in when she listens, ripples with
  each word, turns red when she says stop and gold when she is pleased.
- **The radar** is your whole book around her. Closer means a more recent real conversation; a hot
  lead going cold literally drifts outward. Grey with a slash: leave alone. Red ring: a late promise.
  Tap anyone to ask about them.
- **Before a call:** "Prep me for Gamma" gets what they want, what you promised (deliver that first),
  what you already told them, and the one question still missing.
- **After a call:** say what happened in your own words. She reads back what she will file (promises
  with dates, budget, area, unit, payment, personal details, holds) and files it only when you say
  "file it". If you just told a client a different price than last time, she says so.
- **About to slip:** "I'm calling Beta" after three unanswered messages gets "Don't." Ask again and
  she pushes harder. "I'll tell Alpha 4 million" after you said 4.5 gets "Careful."

She is deterministic on purpose: every sentence comes from what is recorded, so she cannot make up
a price or a promise. Free conversation with a language model is a later owner decision.

## Where she lives
On the owner's computer and phone, the same Donna on both. The app installs to the phone's home
screen. Reaching it away from home needs it hosted somewhere — an owner decision (deployment is
not permitted today) — and real client data stays out until the trial-readiness gate (G6).
